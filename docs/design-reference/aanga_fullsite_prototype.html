Frequently Asked Questions

Home›FAQ
How can we help?
How do I track my order?
You can track your order by visiting the "Track Order" page and entering your Order ID. You can also find your Order ID in your order confirmation email or SMS. For real-time updates, click "Track" in the Orders section of your dashboard.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 7-day easy return and exchange policy on all products. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original packaging. Simply raise a return request from your dashboard and our team will arrange a free pickup within 24 hours.
Do you offer wholesale pricing for shops?
Yes! AANGA has a dedicated wholesale programme for clothing shop owners. Minimum order is 12 pieces per style. Contact us at +91-8677018085 or use the Wholesale Inquiry page to get started. We offer competitive bulk pricing, GST invoices, and pan-India delivery.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard), Net Banking, Mobile Wallets, EMI, and Cash on Delivery (COD). COD is available for orders up to ₹10,000.
How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery across Bihar takes 3–5 business days. Pan-India delivery takes 5–10 business days. Express delivery is available in select cities. Free shipping is available on all orders above ₹999.
Are the products authentic and quality-checked?
Absolutely. Every garment at AANGA passes our rigorous 5-point quality check covering fabric, stitching, print, fit, and finishing. We work directly with trusted manufacturers and stand behind every product we sell.
Can I cancel my order after placing it?
Yes, orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement before they are confirmed by the seller. After that, you may need to wait for delivery and then initiate a return. To cancel, go to My Orders in your dashboard.
Still have questions?

Our team is available Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 8 PM
